## 🏆 Winner, Best Use of MongoDB Atlas, Junction 2026

**[Kitchen Buyer](https://github.com/Afro666/2Brothers)**, built with Tymon and Maciek as team 2Brothers at Junction 2026 in Espoo (13 to 15 November 2026).

A professional kitchen says what it needs. An AI buyer finds the products, checks live prices, stock and order deadlines at the wholesaler, and proposes an order. A person approves every order, and every step is in an audit log.

- **The model never sets a quantity.** It picks products; a purchasing engine sizes every line.
- **Every offer and receipt is signed**, so an agent's price and stock claims can be checked.
- **MongoDB Atlas holds it all**: the catalog, search, orders, a hash-chained audit log, history and forecasts.
- **Open to any agent**: REST, OpenAPI and MCP.

[Live demo](https://kitchenbuyer.pl) · [Code](https://github.com/Afro666/2Brothers)
